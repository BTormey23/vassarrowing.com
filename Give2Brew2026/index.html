How to give

Four strokes

  1. Go to the giving pageengage.vassar.edu/portal/giving_day, Tue 8 AM to Wed 1 PM ET.
  2. Choose Men's and Women's RowingThat's rowing's team choice on the form, so your gift counts toward our 1,000.
  3. Give what you can$5 counts. Gave last year? Please match it.
  4. Bring your peopleGive for your spouse and kids, in their names. Each one counts. Then text a teammate.
Does $5 really count?Yes. Every donor counts as one toward 1,000, whatever the amount.
Can I give in my spouse's or kids' names?Yes, and each person counts separately. One household can be four or five donors.
What do the gifts fund?Needed equipment and team support for Vassar Rowing. Vassar directs 80% of each team's Day of Giving gifts to the team and 20% to the athletics department, which supports all student-athletes.
Already given?Thank you. Forward this page to one teammate who hasn't, and watch for the next milestone.
Who's my captain?Alumni volunteers are captaining each group of class years. Watch for a note from yours. Want to help? Raise your hand.
Giving stock, a DAF, or something bigger?Vassar's Advancement office handles every kind of gift. Reach out and we'll connect you.
